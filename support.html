Support

Need help with Sujeo? Email support@sujeo.app and include your iPhone model and iOS version if you can.

Common questions

Where are my recipes stored?

On your iPhone only. Deleting the app deletes your recipes, so there is no cloud copy to restore from. Sync across devices may come in a future update.

A link did not import. What now?

Some sites block apps, require a login, or do not publish a recipe in a readable form. Try the site’s print view, copy the recipe text into a new recipe, or scan a screenshot. Links to videos only import when the description contains the recipe.

Why is voice control not responding?

Check that Microphone and Speech Recognition are allowed for Sujeo in iOS Settings, and that your iPhone language is set to English.

How do I replay the intro tour?

Open Settings in Sujeo and tap Replay the tour.
